Mentors & Success Stories

Learn from people a few steps ahead of you and, when you are ready, offer support to others.

Success Stories

From campus projects to junior developer

Sarah N. · Kampala · ICT

“I finished my diploma with only class projects. I improved my HTML and CSS through free online practice, built two small sites for community groups, and used those as my portfolio. After three months of applying I got a junior role. The CV tips and interview examples on sites like this helped me stop freezing in interviews.”

Internship that became a full-time offer

David O. · Jinja · Agriculture

“I took an unpaid internship with a cooperative because I wanted field experience. I showed up early, kept good records, and taught myself the mobile tools they used. After the internship they created a paid junior position. My advice: treat every short opportunity as a long interview.”

Building skills while searching

Amina K. · Mbarara · Mixed

“While looking for work I completed two short digital-skills courses and volunteered at a youth centre two afternoons a week. Those experiences gave me concrete examples for interviews and showed employers I was active. I eventually found a data-support role in a health project.”
