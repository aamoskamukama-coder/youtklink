Skills gaps we focus on

Based on labour-market discussions and the needs expressed by youth and employers, the programmes above prioritise:

  • Practical digital skills (web, office tools, online communication)
  • Job-search and workplace readiness
  • Sector-specific skills in agriculture, health support roles and customer-facing work
  • Basic financial literacy and record-keeping

Sources informing this focus include UBOS labour-market findings and conversations with training providers (see Project 1 brief).
