Career Guidance

Practical, locally relevant advice to help you present yourself well and search for work more effectively.

CV & Cover Letter Tips

A good CV for most entry-level roles in Uganda is 1-2 pages, clear, and focused on what you can offer.

  • Contact details _ Name, phone, professional email and portfolio if relevant.
  • Profile / objective _ 2-3 lines stating the role you want and your strongest strength.
  • Education _ Most recent first, with relevant projects or coursework.
  • Experience _ Include internships, volunteer work and part-time jobs.
  • Skills _ Match the job advert and be honest.

Interview Preparation

  • Research the organisation and recent news.
  • Prepare short examples of problem-solving, teamwork and learning.
  • Dress neatly and arrive early or test your internet and camera for online interviews.
  • Prepare 1-2 questions for the interviewer.
  • Send a short thank-you message if you have an email contact.

About YouthLink Uganda

A student project built to reduce the information gap that keeps many young Ugandans from finding work and skills opportunities.

Our mission

Every year hundreds of thousands of young people enter the Ugandan labour market, yet many struggle to discover the limited opportunities that do exist. YouthLink Uganda brings jobs, skills programmes, practical guidance and mentorship connections into one simple, mobile-friendly website.

What you will find here

  • Jobs & Internships _ Entry-level vacancies and practical placements.
  • Skills & Training _ Free and low-cost programmes that improve employability.
  • Career Guidance _ CV tips, interview advice and sector overviews.
  • Mentors & Stories _ Real examples of progress and guidance.
